TMJ Therapy (Temporomandibular / Jaw Joint)

Treatment for jaw pain, clicking, locking and the headaches and neck tension that often go with them.

Book Now Call
About this treatment

What to expect

The jaw is closely linked to the neck and posture. We combine gentle manual therapy, exercises and self-care strategies to ease the joint and surrounding muscles.

Our clinicians have experience treating TMJ disorders as part of a whole-body approach.

Commonly helps with

  • Jaw pain & clicking
  • Difficulty chewing or opening wide
  • Tension headaches
  • Teeth grinding strain
  • Neck & facial tension

FAQ

TMJ Therapy questions

Can physiotherapy help TMJ pain?

Yes. Manual therapy, exercises and posture work can reduce jaw and neck strain for many people.

Do I need to see my dentist too?

Often a team approach works best. We're happy to coordinate with your dentist.
